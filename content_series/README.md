# Content Series: Small Steps, Real Life

## Concept

An eight-post YouTube Shorts series about relatable everyday struggles: study
pressure, procrastination, work anxiety, money stress, relationships, burnout,
social anxiety, and recovery. Each post uses one variable hook and one clear
behavioral takeaway.

## Target audience

Students and early-career adults aged 18- thirty who recognize themselves in
practical mental-health and productivity situations.

## Experimental design

The series is a creative artifact and proposed test subject, not evidence that
any treatment caused engagement. Keep topic, visual style, caption length, and
call-to-action consistent where possible. Record first-party views, likes,
comments, shares, saves, retention, and follower change at fixed intervals in
the posting log.

The primary outcome is engagement rate. Secondary outcomes are share rate,
save rate, comment rate, retention, and relatable-comment rate. Compare matched
pairs only after the planned observation window.

## Hypotheses

- Personal first-person hooks will increase relatable-comment rate.
- A concrete one-step takeaway will increase saves.
- Emotional recognition framing will increase comments without requiring a
  negative sentiment.
- Short videos with a clear first-second hook will be easier to compare across
  the series.

Scripts are in `scripts/`; planned metadata is in `posting_log.csv`. Actual
publication and performance fields must be filled from first-party analytics.
